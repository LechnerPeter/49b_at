## Goals

- Recycle 200x21.5x2 cm and 100x21.5x2 cm stone slabs left over because I mismeasured the outdoor window sills
- A garden table built to last forever
- Can be taken apart

## Design

### Stone slabs

- Dimensions are fixed
- 2 short slabs make up one long one
- 7 long + 6 short → 2 tables: one L S L S L, one L L S L L (L = long slab, S = 2 short slabs)
- Width: 5 × 21.5 cm + 4 × 0.5 cm gap = 109.5 cm

### Metal frame

- Electro-galvanized
- Laser-cut and bent
- Bolted connections (M5)
- Longitudinal
  - 2×
  - 4 mm steel
  - U-profile
- Cross members
  - 7× (important: one in the middle so the short slabs are supported at their ends)
  - The 2 outer ones have tabs so the slabs can't slide off
  - 3 mm steel
  - L-profile
